Privacy

Short version: this site does not run ads or tracking cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or track you across sites. If you choose to sign up for the free printable pack, we keep your email address so we can send it to you.

What this site collects

Homestead Guides is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not run advertising or tracking cookies. To see which guides are useful, each page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-focused service that counts page views without cookies, without storing your IP address for tracking and without following you to other sites; we only see totals such as page views and referring sites. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, and the site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Email signup

If you sign up for the free Homestead Starter Pack, your email address is stored with our email service provider, which sends you the download link and occasional updates about new guides and printables. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to the contact address below.

Links to other sites

Articles link to our Etsy shop (TheHomesteadSOPs) and to public university extension resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices.
