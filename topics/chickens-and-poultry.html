Chickens & Poultry

Raising backyard chickens, ducks, turkeys and quail: coops, brooding, winter laying, hatching eggs and meat birds.

Poultry is where most homesteads start, and it's where small mistakes cost the most: a coop that's a few square feet too small, chicken wire where hardware cloth belongs, a brooder that runs ten degrees too cool.

These guides cover laying hens, ducks, turkeys, quail and meat chickens with the numbers extension poultry programs publish: space per bird, brooder temperatures by week, feed by age, and the predator-proofing details that actually hold.
