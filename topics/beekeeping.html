Beekeeping

Starting your first hive, building a Langstroth box, harvesting honey and getting a colony through winter.

A first beehive is mostly about timing: when to get bees, when to add boxes, when the honey is really ready, and when it's already too late to treat for mites before winter.

These guides walk through starting a hive, building your own Langstroth boxes, harvesting and extracting honey, and getting a colony through a northern winter.
