Goats, Sheep, Pigs & Cows

Small-farm livestock for beginners: dairy and meat goats, sheep, pigs, a family milk cow, meat rabbits and home dairy.

Goats, sheep, pigs and a family cow can turn a few acres into milk, meat and fibre, but each species has one or two rules that beginners learn the hard way, like the copper that's safe for goats and deadly for sheep.

Start here for breed choice, housing, fencing, feeding and health basics, all drawn from university extension research, plus home dairy and processing guides for when the freezer starts filling up.
