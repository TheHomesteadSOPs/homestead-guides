Food Preservation & Kitchen

Canning, fermenting, dehydrating, root cellaring, curing meat and baking bread, with safe numbers from food-safety sources.

Preserving the harvest is the one homestead skill where the exact numbers really matter. Acidity, headspace, processing time and altitude decide whether a jar is safe, not the recipe's age or how good it looks.

These guides cover water-bath and pressure canning, fermenting, dehydrating, root cellaring, curing and smoking meat, safe cooking temperatures and bread baking, with every figure taken from food-safety and extension sources.
