Building & Off-Grid Living

Cabins, sheds, cob ovens, hunting blinds, wood heat, composting toilets and getting the homestead ready for winter.

Homestead building projects reward planning: reading the site before you dig, sizing a foundation for rock or soil, and knowing the safety rules when you're working alone or heating a small space.

Here you'll find guides for cabins, sheds, cob ovens, hunting blinds, composting toilets, firewood and wood heat, an emergency binder, and a full winterizing checklist for the homestead.
